# Design

## Audience
*Written 24 Nov 2026, before generating any picture.*

Ethiopians, especially young people and the Ethiopian diaspora around the world. They will mostly watch on their phones (so vertical first) and share it with family. They know the coffee ceremony and the elders' sayings; many abroad miss them.

## Intent
I want viewers to feel proud of their heritage, connected to their homeland, inspired by their culture, and reminded that no matter how far they travel or how much the world changes, they will always carry their roots with them.

## The story in 3–5 sentences
*Chosen from three options (one object across three generations, a father's letter, the three rounds of a coffee ceremony): the father's letter, because a warm father's voice carries the emotion and lets every theme appear as his memory.*

A son abroad asks his father what Ethiopia is. From his home in Addis Ababa, the father answers by showing what his own father once showed him: the mountains that keep the ancestors' story, a church carved from rock, the elders' wisdom under the warka tree, and the coffee ceremony that gathers the family. Then he turns to the present: his son's generation, building a new Ethiopia. He ends: wherever you go, you carry us; you carry your roots.

No real person's face or voice appears: the father, grandfather and son are fictional, and the narration is a synthetic voice.
