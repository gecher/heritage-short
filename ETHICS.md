# Ethics

## Consent record
No real person is shown or voiced in this film.
- **The father (Abba), the grandfather and the son are fictional.** Their faces were generated by
  FLUX from a written description, not from any photo of a real person.
- **The voice is synthetic** (Gemini TTS, prebuilt voice "Charon"). No real person's voice was
  recorded, cloned or imitated.
- **The Amharic narration** was written and checked by me, a native Amharic speaker.
- Places shown (the highlands, a rock-hewn church, Addis Ababa) are generated, not photographs.

## Labelling plan
- **AI end card in the film:** "Made with AI: images FLUX, voice Gemini TTS. All characters are fictional."
- Platform AI-disclosure switched on when posting.
- No watermarks or provenance metadata removed.

## Private data
- No family names, photos, addresses or voices were sent to any model. The story is about a
  fictional family, so nothing private was needed.
- The free Gemini tier may use inputs to improve Google's products, which is another reason only
  invented content and public cultural facts were sent.

## Cultural care
- The film is for Ethiopian viewers, so cultural mistakes matter. Image prompts describe objects
  instead of naming them (FAILURES F10), and an automatic judge flags anything an Ethiopian viewer
  would find wrong. I review every chosen frame myself before the final cut.
- The film celebrates shared heritage (the coffee ceremony, elders, highlands, Lalibela-style
  churches) and avoids political, ethnic or religious claims.
